Privacy Policy

Marvel Hero Rush Deck Builder · Last updated: 2026-10-11 · Contact: aaronht88@gmail.com

This fan-made, non-commercial deck builder (“the App”) helps players build Marvel Hero Rush TCG decks in the browser. It is not affiliated with Marvel or Card Fun.

1. Data we process

  • Local only (default): deck lists, favorites, and language preference are stored in your browser localStorage. You can use the App fully without signing in.
  • Optional Google sign-in: if you choose to sign in, the App uses Google Identity Services to obtain an ID token. We use your Google account identifier (sub) and email (to show who is signed in). We do not request Google Drive access.
  • Optional cloud sync: when sync is enabled, your deck builder JSON (decks, favorites, language, timestamps) is stored on our Cloudflare Workers / D1 database, keyed by your Google sub.
  • Analytics: the hosted site may use GoatCounter (privacy-friendly page counts). It does not sell personal data.

2. How cloud sync data is used

Synced content is only your deck builder settings. It is used to restore the same decks across devices after you sign in. We do not sell, rent, or share your deck data with third parties for advertising.

3. Who can see your data

Local data stays in your browser. Cloud sync data is stored in our D1 database associated with this App. The App author (aaronht88) operates that backend solely to provide sync.

4. Your choices

  • You can use the App without signing in (localStorage + share codes only).
  • You can revoke Google access anytime in Google Account → Third-party access.
  • You can clear browser site data to remove local decks. Contact us if you need cloud sync data deleted for your account.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use Google sign-in if you are not allowed to consent to Google OAuth in your region.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.

7. Contact
